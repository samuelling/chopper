ASHLAND

Privacy policy

Ashland does not collect, transmit or sell any data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the current year and season, the trees you have felled, your journal, photos and map) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. If you enter the year you were born, only that year is kept, also only on your device. It is used as the game's clock and never leaves it.

If you choose to tell the game about your AI use, through a typical-day setting or a daily check-in, only a coarse daily level (None to Very heavy) is stored, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Links

A link in the game (for example to megazine.org) opens in an in-app browser. Websites you visit that way have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

"Start over from Year One" on the title screen clears your saved game. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Ashland collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
