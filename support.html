ASHLAND

Support

Ashland is a quiet walk through Ashland State Park in Massachusetts, one season at a time, made by Sam Ling. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Playing

Left thumb walks (push to the edge to hurry). Drag the right side to look around. The big button swings, casts and reels, or shoots in camera mode; HOP jumps (hold to dive). USE works boats and gates. The phone holds your map, camera and tools. The ☰ button opens the menu.

Each year is a year of your life

The game can use the year you were born as its clock. It is optional; leave it blank and the game assumes you are 40. It is kept only on your device.

Starting over

Choose "Start over from Year One" on the title screen.

Something wrong?

Email samuelling@me.com with your device model and what happened. Ashland also runs in a web browser at linglab.org/chopper.
